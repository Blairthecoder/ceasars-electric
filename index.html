FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything homeowners and businesses ask us most. Still not sure? Call or text 337-309-4115.

Is Ceasar’s Electric licensed and insured?+
Yes. Ceasar’s Electric is fully licensed, insured, and bonded, and every job is performed by a licensed electrician. We pull permits whenever the work requires one so the job is fully to code.
What areas do you serve?+
We are based in Lake Charles and serve all of Calcasieu Parish plus surrounding communities in Cameron, Jefferson Davis, and Allen parishes — including Sulphur, Westlake, Prien, Moss Bluff, Iowa, Vinton, Jennings, Kinder, and Grand Lake.
Do you offer same-day and emergency service?+
Yes. We offer same-day service across the Lake Charles area when capacity allows, and 24/7 availability for electrical emergencies like burning smells, sparks, or a partial power loss that is not a utility outage.
Do you handle residential, commercial, and industrial work?+
All three. From a single outlet or panel upgrade in a home, to tenant improvements and troubleshooting for businesses, to industrial service work, it is the same license and the same professional standard.
Is my estimate really free?+
Your estimate is included at no additional charge when you book the quoted work. Ask about diagnostic or service-call pricing for troubleshooting visits. We look at the work in person, then put a clear, written quote on paper before anything gets installed.
Do you install and hook up generators?+
Yes. We install whole-home and portable generator connections with proper transfer switches or interlocks, sized for hurricane season, so your home stays powered through an outage.
How do I know if I need a panel upgrade?+
Common signs include breakers that trip often, flickering lights, a warm or buzzing panel, a fuse box or an older Federal Pacific or Zinsco panel, or plans to add an EV charger, larger AC, or a generator. We size a new 200-amp panel to your real load.
Do you pull permits and handle inspections?+
When the local authority requires a permit, we file it under our Louisiana license, schedule the rough and final inspections, and walk the work with the inspector so you receive a finished, code-compliant job.
Do you install EV chargers?+
Yes. We install the dedicated 240-volt circuits and Level 2 charger connections that home EV charging needs, sized correctly and protected at the panel.
What kind of panels and equipment do you work with?+
We work with modern breaker panels and service equipment, replace outdated Federal Pacific, Zinsco, and fuse panels, and install whole-home Type 2 surge protection, generators, and dedicated circuits using quality, code-compliant materials.
