Grand Lake stretches along the water in northern Cameron Parish, and Ceasar’s Electric serves this tight-knit community with licensed, insured electrical work. Owned by Jevante Ceasar, we bring the same care to every Grand Lake job: honest diagnosis, clean terminations, and results done fully to code.

Grand Lake is true coastal Louisiana, so many homes are elevated on piers and flood-zone construction is the norm. We wire and protect service equipment for raised structures, install weatherproof and GFCI circuits, and handle the grounding and surge protection that a coastal, storm-exposed property demands.

Few places take hurricanes more seriously than Cameron Parish. We keep Grand Lake families powered with whole-home and portable generator hookups, transfer switches, and fast storm-damage response, with careful utility coordination for every reconnection. Local and same-day available.
