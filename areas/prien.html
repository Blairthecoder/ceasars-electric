Prien is one of Lake Charles’s most established residential areas, wrapped around Prien Lake and the Country Club Road corridor south of town. Ceasar’s Electric is only minutes away for homeowners near Prien Lake Mall, Nelson Road, and Lake Street. Owner Jevante Ceasar brings licensed, insured workmanship to every job: clean terminations, correct load calculations, and code-compliant results.

Prien mixes stately older homes with newer construction, so we see everything from tired 1960s and 70s service panels to large modern loads that need 200-amp service, dedicated circuits, and whole-home surge protection. Renovations and additions are common here, and we handle the re-wires, service changes, and lighting upgrades that come with them.

Because so much of Prien sits close to Prien Lake and the Calcasieu, we keep flood-zone awareness front of mind: raised meter bases, properly elevated equipment, and storm-ready generator installs. When hurricane season arrives we are local and same-day available for standby generators, service repairs, and storm-damage response, fed by Entergy or a rural cooperative alike.

Do you serve the Prien and Prien Lake area?+
Yes. Prien is just south of our Lake Charles base, so we are minutes away with same-day service and 24/7 emergency availability for the Country Club Road, Nelson Road, and Prien Lake neighborhoods.
My Prien home has an older panel — should I upgrade?+
Many established Prien homes still run undersized or aging panels. If you are adding AC capacity, an EV charger, or a generator, or if you see flickering and tripping, a 200-amp upgrade is usually the safe, code-compliant move. Your estimate is free when you book.
Do you install generators before hurricane season in Prien?+
Yes. We install transfer switches and wire whole-home and portable generators, with equipment elevated appropriately for lake-adjacent, flood-aware properties.
