Carlyss sits just south of Sulphur, and Ceasar’s Electric is only a short drive away for homeowners along Choupique Road, Grand Lake Road, and the neighborhoods between Highway 27 and the industrial corridor. Owned by Jevante Ceasar, we bring licensed, insured electrical work to Carlyss with the same standard on every call: correct diagnosis, clean terminations, and work done fully to code.

Many Carlyss homes are 1970s-and-later builds on larger lots, and we regularly see undersized 100-amp panels, tired branch wiring, and detached shops and outbuildings that need properly sized, grounded sub-panels. With Sasol, PPG, and the Highway 90 plants next door, we also handle the surge protection and grounding upgrades that matter when you live near heavy industry.

This is Gulf Coast country, so storm load is part of every conversation. Whether you are adding a standby generator before hurricane season, replacing a service mast the wind took down, or upgrading a panel for a new AC system, we are local and same-day available. Homes fed by Entergy or a rural cooperative both get careful utility coordination.
