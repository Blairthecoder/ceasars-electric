Welsh is a close-knit rice-farming town in Jefferson Davis Parish, and Ceasar’s Electric is glad to serve its homeowners, farms, and small businesses. Owned by Jevante Ceasar, we bring licensed, insured workmanship to Welsh: honest diagnosis, clean terminations, and work done to code.

Welsh properties range from older homes in town to farms and acreage on the edges, so we handle everything from panel upgrades and re-wires to agricultural sub-panels, well-pump circuits, and shop wiring. Older service equipment and undersized panels are common, and we bring them up to modern, safe standards.

When storms roll across the prairie, we are ready with standby generator installs, transfer switches, and quick storm-damage repairs. Local, licensed, and same-day available, with coordination for Jeff Davis Electric Cooperative and other providers.
