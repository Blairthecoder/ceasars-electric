EV Charger Installation in Lake Charles, LA

Convenient Level 2 charging, correctly sized for your home and panel.

Ceasar’s Electric installs home and commercial electric vehicle charging circuits throughout Lake Charles and Calcasieu Parish. A Level 2 charger can add substantially more driving range than a standard receptacle, but it must be matched to the charger, vehicle, wiring method, and available electrical capacity.

We evaluate the panel, perform the necessary load calculation, plan the route, and install the dedicated 240-volt circuit and required protection. If the existing service is near capacity, we explain the available options before installation.

What’s Included

✓Electrical panel and capacity evaluation
✓Load calculation for the proposed charger
✓Dedicated 240-volt branch circuit
✓Hardwired or receptacle-based charger connections
✓Indoor or outdoor installation planning
✓Permitting and inspection coordination when required

Why Customers Choose Ceasar’s Electric

An EV charger is a continuous electrical load. Correct conductor sizing, breaker selection, grounding, protection, and equipment placement are essential for reliable daily charging.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What size circuit does a Level 2 EV charger need?+
The circuit depends on the charger’s maximum output, the vehicle, and the available panel capacity. We size the breaker and conductors to the actual equipment and applicable electrical code.
Do I need a panel upgrade before installing an EV charger?+
Not always. We perform a load calculation first. Some homes have sufficient capacity, while others may need a service upgrade or an approved load-management solution.
Can the charger be installed outdoors?+
Many chargers are listed for outdoor use. We confirm the equipment rating and use a wiring method and placement suitable for the installation location.
Do you install customer-supplied chargers?+
We can evaluate customer-supplied equipment for compatibility and listing. Share the model information when requesting a quote so we can plan the correct circuit.
