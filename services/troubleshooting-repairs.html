FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

My breaker keeps tripping. What does that mean?+
A breaker that trips repeatedly is protecting you from an overload or a fault. We trace the circuit to find the true cause rather than just resetting it, then repair it safely.
Do you charge for a diagnosis?+
We give you a clear quote before any work begins, and the estimate is included at no additional charge when you book the quoted repair.
How fast can you come out?+
We offer same-day service across Lake Charles and Calcasieu Parish, with 24/7 availability for electrical emergencies.
What should I do if I smell something burning near an outlet?+
Turn off the circuit at the panel if you safely can, unplug what you can, and call a licensed electrician right away. A burning smell is a serious fire hazard.
