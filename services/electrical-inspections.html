FAQ

Frequently asked questions

When should I schedule an electrical inspection?+
Consider an inspection for an older property, before major renovations, after storm or water damage, when buying a home, or when breakers, outlets, and lights show recurring problems.
Is this the same as a municipal inspection?+
No. We provide a contractor safety evaluation and repair recommendations. Official permit inspections are performed by the authority having jurisdiction.
Can you correct the problems found during the inspection?+
Yes. We can provide a separate repair or upgrade scope for the items identified and coordinate permits when required.
Will you inspect a Federal Pacific or Zinsco panel?+
Yes. We identify the panel type, visible condition, service capacity, and replacement considerations, then explain the appropriate options.
