FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What counts as an electrical emergency?+
Sparks, smoke, burning odors, hot panels, exposed energized wiring, repeated arcing, or a partial power loss with signs of damage should be treated as urgent.
What should I do if I smell burning near an outlet or panel?+
If you can do so safely, stop using the affected circuit and move away from the area. Call 911 if there is smoke or fire, then contact a licensed electrician.
Should I keep resetting a breaker that trips?+
No. A breaker that repeatedly trips is responding to an overload or fault. Repeatedly resetting it can worsen damage and should not replace a proper diagnosis.
