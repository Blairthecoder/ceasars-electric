Panel Upgrades

Modern breaker panels built for the way you use power today.

An outdated or overloaded electrical panel is one of the most common causes of tripped breakers, flickering lights, and fire risk in older Lake Charles homes. Ceasar’s Electric replaces aging panels with modern, code-compliant service equipment sized for today’s electrical demand.

We handle everything from 100A to 200A-plus upgrades, including hazardous Federal Pacific and Zinsco swap-outs, with the permit and utility coordination handled for you.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a panel upgrade cost in Lake Charles?+
Cost depends on your current service size, the panel location, and whether the meter or service entrance also needs work. After a quick look we give you a clear, written quote, and the estimate is included at no additional charge when you book the quoted work.
How long does a panel upgrade take?+
Most residential panel upgrades are completed in a single day. We coordinate the utility disconnect and reconnect so your power is off for the shortest time possible.
Do I need a permit to replace my electrical panel?+
Yes, panel replacements require a permit and inspection. We handle the permit and utility coordination for you so the work is fully to code.
Should I upgrade to 200-amp service?+
Most modern homes are safer and more comfortable with 200-amp service, especially if you are adding an EV charger, a larger AC system, or a generator. We size your panel to your real electrical demand.
