Electrical Emergency in Lake Charles? What to Do Before the Electrician Arrives

JC
Jevante Ceasar
Owner, Ceasar’s Electric. November 15, 2026

Electrical problems escalate quickly. The first few minutes matter, and what you do before help arrives can keep a bad situation from becoming a fire or an injury.

Call 911 First If You See

  • Flames or smoke from an outlet, panel, or appliance
  • A downed power line on or near your home
  • Someone has been shocked and is hurt

Call 911, get everyone out, and call an electrician after the fire department clears the scene.

Call an Emergency Electrician For

  • A burning or hot-plastic smell with no visible flame
  • Sparks, buzzing, or crackling from outlets, switches, or the panel
  • A breaker panel that is warm or hot to the touch
  • Scorched or melted outlets and switch plates
  • Breakers that trip repeatedly and will not stay reset
  • Partial power loss or lights that flicker across several rooms
  • Storm damage to the meter, mast, or service entrance

What to Do Right Now

  1. Stop using the affected circuit. Unplug what you can safely reach.
  2. Shut off power if it is safe. If the panel is dry, cool, and not sparking, switch off the affected breaker or the main.
  3. Stay out of standing water. Never touch electrical equipment while wet.
  4. Do not reset a breaker repeatedly. A breaker that keeps tripping is protecting you from a real fault.
  5. Call 337-309-4115. Tell us what you saw, smelled, or heard, and which part of the home is affected.

After the Emergency

Once power is safe, ask for the cause, not just the repair. Overloaded circuits, loose connections, and aging panels tend to fail again. A follow-up electrical inspection can find the rest of the problem. Our emergency electrician service covers Lake Charles, Sulphur, Westlake, and surrounding areas, and the signs of a failing panel are worth knowing before something goes wrong.

Frequently Asked Questions

What counts as an electrical emergency?

Burning smell, visible sparks, a hot or buzzing panel, scorched outlets, partial power loss with flickering, or a downed service line. If you see fire or smoke, call 911 first.

Should I turn off the main breaker?

If you can reach the panel safely and it is dry, turning off the main breaker removes power from the house. Do not touch a panel if you are standing in water or the panel is hot, sparking, or scorched.

Do you offer 24-hour electrician service in Lake Charles?

Yes. Our emergency line is open 24/7 at 337-309-4115 for urgent electrical problems.

What about a power outage that is only on my house?

Check the main breaker and your neighbors first. If neighbors have power, it is likely your service, meter, or panel, and that is a call for an electrician.
