Electrical Panel Upgrade Cost in Lake Charles, LA

JC
Jevante Ceasar
Owner, Ceasar’s Electric. November 15, 2026

Panel upgrade pricing is one of the most searched electrical questions in Southwest Louisiana, and one of the hardest to answer without seeing the panel. Two homes that both say "200-amp upgrade" can differ by thousands of dollars in scope.

Here is what actually sets the price, so you can compare quotes on equal terms.

What Drives the Cost of a Panel Upgrade

  • Panel swap vs. full service change. Replacing the panel only is the lowest-cost version. If the meter base, service mast, weatherhead, or service conductors also need replacing, the job becomes a service change.
  • Amp rating. Moving from 100 amps to 200 amps changes the wire size, breakers, and often the meter base. Homes with EV chargers, central HVAC, or electric ranges usually need the larger size.
  • Distance and routing. Panels on the far side of a house from the meter, or in finished spaces, take more labor to feed.
  • Condition of existing wiring. Double-tapped breakers, aluminum branch wiring, or damaged conductors found at the panel have to be corrected before the new panel goes in.
  • Brand and hardware. Panel brand, breaker types (AFCI and GFCI where required), and whole-home surge protection add to the material list.
  • Permits and utility coordination. Service work requires a permit, an inspection, and coordination with the utility for disconnect and reconnect.

100-Amp vs. 200-Amp Service

A 100-amp panel can run a small home with gas appliances. Most Lake Charles homes now run central air, electric water heaters, and sometimes pools or workshops, and 200-amp service is the usual target. If you plan to add an EV charger or a generator connection, size the panel for that load now rather than upgrading twice.

Signs You Are Paying for the Right Scope

  • The quote is in writing and lists amp rating, panel brand, meter base, and permit.
  • The electrician inspected the existing panel and meter before quoting.
  • Surge protection is itemized, not assumed.
  • The price states what happens if hidden problems are found behind the old panel.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does an electrical panel upgrade cost in Lake Charles?

The price depends on the amp rating you need, whether the meter base and service mast must be replaced, the distance from the meter to the panel, and permit and utility coordination. A panel swap with existing service is the low end. A full service change is the high end. We give a written quote after looking at your panel.

How long does a panel upgrade take?

Most panel swaps are completed in one day. Jobs that include a new meter base, mast, or utility disconnect take longer because they depend on the utility and inspection schedule.

Do I need a permit to upgrade my panel?

Yes. Panel and service work in Calcasieu Parish and surrounding parishes is permitted and inspected. We handle the permit and inspection scheduling as part of the job.

Will I lose power during the upgrade?

Yes, for part of the day. We schedule the outage and plan the work so power is off as briefly as possible.

The Short Version

The cheapest quote is rarely the same job as the next one. Compare scope first: panel only or full service change, amp rating, permit, surge protection, and what is included if the old wiring needs correction. We serve Lake Charles, Sulphur, Westlake, and the rest of Calcasieu Parish.
