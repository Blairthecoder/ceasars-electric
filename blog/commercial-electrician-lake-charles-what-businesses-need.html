Commercial Electrician in Lake Charles: What Businesses Should Look For

JC
Jevante Ceasar
Owner, Ceasar’s Electric. November 15, 2026

When the power goes out at a business, the cost is measured in lost sales and idle staff, not just repair time. Choosing a commercial electrician before there is a problem is one of the cheapest forms of insurance a Lake Charles business owner has.

What to Look for in a Commercial Electrician

  • Licensed, insured, and bonded. Ask for proof, especially if you lease and the landlord requires it.
  • Commercial experience. Three-phase service, larger panels, and commercial code requirements are not the same as residential work.
  • Scheduling that protects your hours. The electrician should plan shutdowns around when you are open.
  • A real emergency line. Outages do not follow business hours.
  • Written quotes. Scope, permits, and timeline in writing before work starts.

Work Businesses Commonly Need

  • Panel and service upgrades for added equipment or tenant build-outs
  • Lighting retrofits, parking lot and exterior lighting. See our guide to outdoor lighting installation
  • Dedicated circuits for kitchen equipment, medical equipment, and machinery
  • Troubleshooting repeat breaker trips and power quality problems
  • Generator connections so the business stays open after storms
  • Pre-lease and code-correction inspections

Industries We Work With

Requirements differ by building type. See how we approach restaurants, retail, offices, medical and dental, warehouses and industrial, and property management.

Reduce Downtime Before It Happens

  1. Have the panel inspected before peak season, especially if equipment was added.
  2. Label every circuit and keep a panel schedule on site.
  3. Add surge protection at the service to protect POS systems, refrigeration, and HVAC.
  4. Save the emergency number in every manager's phone: 337-309-4115.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does a commercial electrician do that a residential electrician does not?

Commercial work involves three-phase power, larger panels and feeders, code requirements for occupancy type, and work scheduled around business hours. The licensing and experience requirements differ.

Can you work after hours so we do not lose business?

Yes. We schedule disruptive work, such as panel or circuit shutdowns, around your operating hours when the job allows.

Do you handle emergency repairs for businesses?

Yes. Our emergency line is open 24/7 at 337-309-4115 for outages, tripped mains, and other urgent faults.

What types of businesses do you serve?

Restaurants, retail, offices, medical and dental, warehouses and industrial spaces, and property managers across Lake Charles and Calcasieu Parish.
