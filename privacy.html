Privacy Notice

How We Use Your Information

This notice applies to information you send through ceasarselectric.com.

Information You Provide

When you request an estimate, you may provide your name, phone number, project description, service selection, and an optional photo. We use that information to understand your request, contact you, schedule service, and maintain records related to the work.

Calls and Text Messages

If you provide a phone number, Ceasar’s Electric may call or text you about your request. Message and data rates may apply. We do not use your estimate request to enroll you in unrelated marketing messages.

Website Measurement

The site uses Google Analytics to understand page visits and actions such as phone-link clicks and completed estimate requests. This helps us improve the website and understand which services people are looking for.

Sharing and Retention

We use service providers, including our website host and analytics provider, to operate the site and receive requests. We do not sell the personal information submitted through the estimate form. Information is kept only as long as reasonably needed for customer service, business records, and legal obligations.

Your Choices

You can request access, correction, or deletion of information you submitted by emailing ceasarselectric@gmail.com. You can also ask us to stop texting you by replying STOP.
